### what is ambergris
**smollm3:** Ambergris is a soft, waxy, highly fragrant and spicy substance used in perfumery, pastiles, precious candles, hair powders, and pomatum. It is also used in cooking by the Turks and carried to Mecca for the same purpose as frankincense is carried to St. Peter’s in Rome. Some wine merchants drop a few grains into claret to flavor it. It is found in the bowels of a sick whale and is valued at a gold guinea an ounce to any druggist.
