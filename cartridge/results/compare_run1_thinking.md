### what does Tier 1 benefits mean
**smollm3:** Tier 1 benefits mean the percentage of the wage replacement a covered individual is entitled to earn on wages up to fifty percent (50%) of the state average weekly wage. This is calculated by multiplying the state average weekly wage by 50% and rounding up to the nearest whole dollar to determine the Tier 1 Wage Cap. The portion of the individual's average weekly wage that is less than or equal to the Tier 1 Wage Cap is then multiplied by 90% and rounded up to the nearest whole dollar to determine the Tier 1 Benefit Amount.
